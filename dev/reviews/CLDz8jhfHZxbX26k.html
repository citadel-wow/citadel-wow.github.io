Council & Illidan

Illidan Council opening Survival Interrupts Every pull Next raid

The biggest gains are a stable Council opening and safe movement during Illidan's first Eye Blast. Council also needs better poison coverage, control of Veras after Vanish, and less damage taken from ground effects and Reflective Shield. These pulls point to survival and encounter control as the immediate priorities.

Reviewed: BT Council and Illidan tries, the newest log in the guild site's archive. Council died on attempt 14 after 13 wipes; all three Illidan attempts ended in the air phase. Times below are elapsed time within the pull, and WCL fight numbers are provided to make every example easy to find.

Seven Eye Blast deaths; Eye Blast caused the first loss on all three attempts. The late enrage, melee, and fire deaths should not obscure that opening failure. Roarwind's two lethal beam impacts were approximately 20,000 damage including overkill; topping him up alone would not have saved him.

For next raid:

  • Mark the two Flame tank routes and ranged/healer positions before pulling. Walk through how everyone moves for a beam.
  • Assign one caller to announce the beam's observed path and the required movement. Tanks move early enough to keep their Flame facing away from the raid; melee back away when the position becomes unsafe.
  • Keep each Flame near its glaive and avoid fleeing to the room's edge. Separate the ranged/healer groups while preserving healer reach.
  • Give the two Flame tanks explicit healer assignments and a backup for a healer who must move or dies.

These positioning requirements follow the Illidan encounter mechanics. The observed initial failure was the beam, rather than an early Uncaged Wrath.

There is also a useful positive: all three attempts reached the air phase without a player death. Draw Soul hit only Madef in the recorded damage events, and there were no Shear debuff applications. Preserve that phase-one execution and spend the next learning attempts on surviving the first beam with both Flame tanks alive.

2. Council: secure the opening tank assignments and healing

Five of the 13 Council wipes began with a tank death: Madef three times, Roarwind once, and Jonette once. Four of those first deaths happened within 20 seconds.

  • Madef, fight 8, 0:09.5: the final 3.7-second death window contained about 23.5k damage including overkill and only 4.4k healing. The healing events in that window were Prayer of Mending, Renew, Earth Shield, and Leader of the Pack; no direct cast heal landed. This is an opening healing-coverage failure to address with explicit assignments and precasting. Fight 8
  • Madef, fight 9, 0:47.7: his last effective heal landed at 0:42.4, approximately 5.3 seconds before death. The two final melee swings alone dealt about 15.4k including overkill, with Judgment of Blood adding pressure. He needs continuous coverage while healers reposition. Fight 9
  • Madef, fight 11, 0:19.2: a 5.7k Judgment of Command landed between melee swings. No Spell Reflection application was recorded before this death. Pair reliable reflection timing with sustained healing; neither replaces the other. Fight 11
  • Roarwind, fight 12, 0:06.4: Dire Bear Form was removed at 0:05.78, while Veras was actively attacking. The subsequent damage events show armor dropping from 31,764 to 7,626 before the lethal blow. This is a specific, preventable loss of mitigation. Check any shift/consumable macro used there; remain in Bear Form while receiving boss swings. The log does not reveal why the form was cancelled. Fight 12
  • Jonette, fight 29, 0:07.9: Veras was attacking her when Zerevor's Arcane Bolt landed for approximately 13.2k including overkill. Loranoa was alive and had Dampen Magic. This was a wrong-target bolt during the opening, not evidence that Jonette lacked ordinary tank healing or that Loranoa had forgotten the buff. Confirm Zerevor is securely on the mage before the group commits. Fight 29

Use the same countdown, boss marks, hunter-to-tank Misdirection assignments, and named healing assignments on each pull. Confirm all four bosses are controlled before full damage begins. There are Misdirection events in the log; the action is to coordinate the existing tools, not simply add more hunters.

3. Council: stop losing players while the pull is still recoverable

Poison needs planned burst healing. Xurioz was the first death on fight 14 at 0:53.3 to Envenom; Kianne followed at 1:55.8 to Deadly Poison. On the eventual kill, Æturnum still died to Envenom at 0:40.6 and Swellery at 5:51.0. Assign a healer to react to Deadly Poison immediately and keep the target healthy through its final burst. Players should use an available survival consumable early and avoid adding self-damage when already threatened. Do not label this as a missed dispel based only on the poison's name.

Repeated ground-effect ticks are costing players, not just healer mana. Xurioz died on fight 10 after Shadow Word: Death followed by two Blizzard hits. Malfori took the initial Flamestrike and two later ticks before dying on fight 9. On fight 20, Virtuálskill died after three Blizzard hits at 3:30.5, 3:32.5, and 3:34.5; Roarwind then died to Empowered Smite at 3:45.0. Losing a main interrupter made the subsequent situation harder, although the log alone does not prove every Smite could have been covered. Prioritize leaving the effect over finishing a cast or maintaining melee uptime.

Keep Veras off damage dealers after Vanish. Castratos was the first death on fight 17 at 1:03.2 to a Veras melee hit, and Swellery was first on fight 20 at 1:57.1 after two Veras swings. Fight 18 lost Prevaraa, Shcherbaque, and Tarmour to Veras before the tank deaths. Assign a return caller and pickup assistance, with the tank ready for whichever immunity buff Veras has. Exact threat errors or tank positioning cannot be determined from these death sequences alone.

Control damage into Reflective Shield. Cthulha died directly to it on fight 13 at 1:14.0 and fight 14 at 3:27.4. On the kill, two reflections of about 3.6k each were followed by a lethal Arcane Explosion at 1:19.7. Avoid uncontrolled burst into the shield; any deliberate shield damage needs coordinated healing. Keep Zerevor's nearby area clear.

Protect the mage tank's healer coverage. On fight 15, Loranoa was the first death at 1:55.6. Dampen Magic remained active until death. An Arcane Bolt and Arcane Explosion landed during a 9.4-second interval between heals, followed by only a 1.4k heal before the lethal bolt. Swellery died to an unmitigated bolt four seconds later and other healers followed. The improvement here is keeping the mage and healer safely connected, with backup coverage during movement. 4. Council: retain the interrupt team, add dependable backup

Virtuálskill and Swellery did most of the interrupt work. On the successful pull they interrupted 16 Circle of Healing casts between them: 11 and 5 respectively. Two casts still completed, at 3:44.1 and 5:58.5. This is a concrete cleanup target even after the kill.

Have a named physical interrupter, magical interrupter, and backup, with a clear handoff when someone moves, dies, or cannot act through an immunity. Protect Circle of Healing coverage first. Do not use raw damage rankings to criticize players assigned to this job. Successful Council pull

Make the evening smoother

From the first Council pull to the kill took 2:18:22, including 44:48 in Council combat and 1:33:35 between attempts. The median gap between attempts was 5:42. Those gaps include recovery, discussion, and logged trash re-clears; they cannot all be classified as avoidable delay.

After a wipe, have one person identify the first meaningful failure, choose one correction, then start the ready check once resurrection, assignments, buffs, food, and mana are complete. Give repair/reagent checks a fixed place in the reset routine. Saving one minute across each of the 13 Council resets would create roughly 13 additional minutes for Illidan practice; that is a proposed improvement, not a measured amount of wasted time.

For the next run, track five simple outcomes:

  1. No tank deaths in Council's opening 30 seconds.
  2. No uncontrolled boss swings or Arcane Bolts on the wrong players.
  3. No deaths to repeated ground ticks or uncontrolled reflection damage.
  4. No completed Circle of Healing casts.
  5. Both Flame tanks and the full raid survive Illidan's first Eye Blast without a Flame Blast hitting the raid.

The Council kill already demonstrates that the group can sustain the encounter. Repeating that control and surviving Illidan's beam should buy more progress than optimizing personal damage numbers first.
