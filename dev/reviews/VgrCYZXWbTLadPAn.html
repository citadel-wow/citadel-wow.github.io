Fix the tank handoffs before Council

Bloodboil Gathios handoff Council attrition Every wipe Next raid

24 September 2026 · Raid 1 · Warcraft Logs report

Seven kills and five wipes: one on Gurtogg Bloodboil and four on the Illidari Council. Both bosses exposed the same operational weakness: after the active tank lost control or died, the next tank did not have the boss immediately. On Bloodboil that turned a frontal attack into five instant deaths. On Council it left Gathios free to walk through the raid after Madef died.

This review reads the deaths chronologically and checks damage, healing, casts, interrupts, buffs and debuffs around the decisive events. The 100 death-table rows across the five wipes include cleanup and repeat deaths after resurrection; they are not 100 independent mistakes. The log cannot reveal voice calls or healer assignments, so the conclusions below stay with observable events.

Bloodboil: the swap failed, then the frontal hit the raid

Fight 50, 1:45, 71.27% remaining. Roarwind received Bewildering Strike at 0:48.544. The next recorded boss melee targeted Naincapacité at 0:51.769; no Taunt or Growl appears between those events. Twenty-four milliseconds later, Arcing Smash hit six players. Naincapacité, Dipstick, Lurkmisses, Loranoa and Swellery died immediately. Bruand survived the Smash but died to the next Bloodboil tick at 0:52.188. Fight 50 deaths.

Madef acquired the boss by 0:55.011, but the recovery was already six players down. He took a 10,323 melee hit, received one 911 Earth Shield heal, and died to a second melee hit 0.835 seconds later. A 2,033 Prayer of Mending at 0:53.600 was the only other effective healing on him in the preceding two seconds.

Next pull: call each Bewildering Strike and confirm the next tank before damage resumes. The backup must already be in position and high on threat; the raid must remain outside the tank lane even during a target change. If the boss faces a non-tank, stop damage and move away from the frontal until control is restored. Healers should switch to the incoming tank before the first swing, not after it.

Council: Gathios repeatedly had no second tank

Madef tanked Gathios through the meaningful part of all four attempts. Once Madef died, Gathios had no prompt replacement:

  • Fight 74: Madef was the first death at 0:28. Gathios then killed 15 other raiders. Roarwind died to Gathios at 0:59 without establishing a surviving pickup.
  • Fight 75: Madef died at 7:07. Gathios killed five raiders over the next 21 seconds before Roarwind finally held him for 23 seconds. This was the only successful backup pickup.
  • Fight 77: Madef died at 0:09. Jonette was killed by Gathios at 0:34; the boss never settled on a surviving tank.
  • Fight 78: Madef died at 3:03. Gathios killed five players before the pull ended 19 seconds later, with no replacement tank recorded.

The assignment needs a real contingency. Name one backup for Gathios who maintains enough threat and stands close enough to take him immediately. Give that player a dedicated healer and a clear call when Madef is in danger. A tank whom Gathios kills on contact has not completed the handoff.

Madef’s deaths also show two avoidable overlaps. On fight 77, nine seconds into the pull, a 7,344 Gathios melee, 720 Seal of Blood and 1,987 Flamestrike damage landed in the same sequence; only one 724 Earth Shield heal restored health. On fight 75, Madef’s final window included two 4,344 Blizzard ticks followed by Gathios melee. Keep Zerevor’s Blizzard and Flamestrike away from the Gathios position. Fight 78 ended with two Gathios swings around a 2,010 Consecration tick, with 4,678 healing in the death window; that tank needed stronger direct coverage before the second swing.

Council: reduce the losses before the tank falls

The longest pull reached 33.19%, but it lost 13 players before Madef died: seven to Veras, five to Zerevor, and one opening death to Gathios. The sequence included Envenom deaths at 0:56, 1:01, 3:09, 6:42 and 6:47; Flamestrike deaths at 1:07 and 2:45; and Blizzard deaths at 1:53, 6:16 and 6:24. This was prolonged attrition, not a single final healing failure.

Fight 78 showed the same pattern on a shorter clock. Tunaak died to Blizzard at 0:20 and Patbutchers to Flamestrike at 0:27. After a resurrection, Tunaak died again to Envenom at 2:02; Nosaj followed to Envenom at 2:12, then Leeane to Blizzard at 2:50. Those five losses happened before Madef’s death at 3:03.

Movement and poison response are the largest gains. Treat Blizzard and Flamestrike as immediate move calls, including for the Gathios group. Give Veras targets fast direct healing through Deadly Poison and Envenom rather than relying on raid healing. The interrupt work should be preserved: fight 78 recorded 20 interrupts and no completed Circle of Healing cast. On fight 75, 18 Circle of Healing casts were interrupted and three completed; missed Council healing was not the main cause of the 13 earlier deaths.

Next raid: four calls to agree before pulling

  1. Bloodboil swap: call Bewildering Strike, confirm the incoming tank, and stop damage if the boss turns. Nobody stands in the tank-facing lane.
  2. Gathios backup: name the replacement tank and healer. The backup maintains threat and takes Gathios immediately if Madef dies.
  3. Zerevor placement: keep Blizzard and Flamestrike away from Madef and move on the first visual or call.
  4. Veras response: assign fast direct healing for poison targets while keeping the successful Malande interrupt rotation.

The clearest sign of improvement is operational: no boss facing the raid during a Bloodboil swap, no Zerevor floor effect on the Gathios tank, and Gathios on a living backup within seconds of Madef’s loss. Those changes preserve enough players for the existing interrupt work and boss damage to matter.
