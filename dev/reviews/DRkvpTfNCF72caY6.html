Nine wipes: Council protection, Reliquary reflection and Flames

Recent raids Bloodboil Reliquary Council Illidan Every wipe Next raid

29 October 2026 · Raid 1 · Warcraft Logs report

Nine kills, nine wipes: Bloodboil once, Reliquary twice, Council four times and Illidan twice. The largest gains are a reliable Council opening and mage protection, controlled damage during Reliquary’s reflection phase, and safe Flame tank movement on Illidan. These are distinct failures; assigning all nine wipes to healing or damage output would miss the evidence.

This review freshly checks October 22 and 29 per-pull deaths and paginated damage, healing, aura, cast and interrupt events, including the matching kills. Death counts include cleanup and repeat deaths after resurrection, not independent mistakes. Times are relative to each pull.

Bloodboil: a dangerous stack and damage overlap

Fight 56: 2:01, 54.08% remaining. Madef was the first death at 0:54.3. Acidic Wound reached 12 stacks immediately before he died, with melee, Arcing Smash and Fel-Acid Breath contributing to the final window. The death table records 21,482 healing/absorption over 10.51 seconds; this was not a complete absence of healing. Madef had received Bewildering Strike at 0:25.9 and later resumed taking stacks without the earlier stack having cleared.

Fel Rage went onto Littlewhitey at 0:55.1. Gonnadietoo then died to Bloodboil at 0:57.3, Quelva to Fel Arcing Smash at 1:04.8, and Littlewhitey at 1:13.9 during Fel Rage. The raid was losing its tank and healing resources before the later frontal deaths.

Plan the handoff and healing escalation before the current tank becomes unsafe. Keep the next tank ready, track the stack and damage together, and immediately assign direct healing to the Fel Rage target. Do not invent a universal 12-stack failure rule: Madef reached 13 stacks on the next pull and survived the kill. That kill took 2:59 with only two death-table entries; the preceding week’s Bloodboil kill had seven.

Reliquary: clear the approach, then control reflected damage

Fight 60: 0:54, 57.38% encounter health remaining. The first five deaths—Trígger, Cthulha, Kianne, Glimmerdusk and Jonette—were killing blows from Suffering or Hungering Soul Fragments. Essence of Suffering was already active, casting Aura of Suffering at 0:03.2. Approach trash was still attacking players after the encounter began. Clear and collect those fragments before triggering the boss. The zero-healing death windows in this phase should not be treated as normal healer inactivity.

Fight 63: 6:26, 0.55% encounter health remaining. Five players died to Aura of Desire: Gahenna at 2:16.5, Littlewhitey at 2:19.0, Loranoa at 2:21.2, Kalinak at 2:26.7 and Glimmerdusk at 2:52.9. Deaden was applied to the boss at 2:13.6, immediately before the first cluster. Gahenna also took 4,234 Shadow Word: Death recoil in the fatal sequence. This is a reason to coordinate damage with health and healing during reflection, especially when using reflected Deaden.

Six Spirit Shock casts completed on the wipe. The first at 2:35.4 disoriented Madef; Shcherbaque then died to boss melee at 2:37.5. Later, Essence of Anger gained Seethe at 5:08.7 and Madef died at 5:11.3 to rapid melee plus Aura of Anger, despite 7,127 healing/absorption in the final 1.603-second window. The ending percentage hides substantial earlier losses.

The kill gives a practical model: fight 66 had zero Aura of Desire deaths, one completed Spirit Shock, and three interrupted Deaden casts, with no Deaden application to the boss. It finished in 5:15 with two deaths. Use a clear interrupt rotation with a backup, and choose the safer Deaden approach until reflection survival is repeatable. If deliberately reflecting Deaden, call the damage increase and adjust casting to player health; do not keep bursting through lethal self-damage.

Council: four wipes, three different problems

Fight 86: mage protection was not established. Loranoa died to Arcane Bolt at 0:04.2. The first recorded successful Spellsteal/Dampen Magic acquisition was Littlewhitey’s at 0:09.7, after that death. Gonnadietoo died to another Bolt at 0:13.1. Confirm the intended mage has protection and boss control before the raid commits.

Fight 87: floor damage and reflection caused the early losses. Kianne died after two seconds of Blizzard damage at 0:52.6; Thunderzaar died to Reflective Shield at 0:52.8. After resurrection, Thunderzaar died to Blizzard again at 1:20.6. Littlewhitey’s Envenom death also included Blizzard damage; Gahenna and Bruand then died to Blizzard at 1:33.5 and 1:35.5. The full death table contains seven Blizzard and eleven Flamestrike killing blows, including later cleanup. Move immediately and stop attacks that are returning lethal Reflective Shield damage.

Fight 88: protection and target control were separate issues. Loranoa successfully acquired Dampen Magic at 0:00.4, but Zerevor’s Arcane Bolt hit Jonette. Jonette died at 0:07.3 after Arcane Bolt and Malande’s Empowered Smite overlapped, with only 957 healing in the 2.603-second death window. A successful Spellsteal does not by itself prove that every boss is attacking the assigned tank. Later Divine Intervention deaths are reset/cleanup actions, not new execution failures.

Fight 89: a gap in the mage’s protection preceded the collapse. Loranoa’s Dampen Magic expired at 2:00.874. Zerevor cast Arcane Bolt at Loranoa at 2:05.045. Loranoa reacquired Dampen Magic at 2:06.214, but the Bolt still killed him at 2:07.0. Several earlier Spellsteal casts did not produce a recorded successful refresh. Track the actual buff and successful steal, not just the button press. Once Loranoa died, Arcane Bolts killed Gahenna, Quelva and Snney; Madef’s death window also included Arcane Bolt damage alongside Gathios melee.

Fight 91 then killed Council in 5:02 with only one death, to Flamestrike. Malande completed one Circle of Healing while 13 were interrupted; the longer wipes completed one and two respectively. Preserve the interrupt rotation, but the chronological failures point first to mage survival, boss targeting and movement. Council’s October 22 kill had 12 death-table entries: the latest successful pull was substantially cleaner.

Illidan: Eye Blast and an enraged Flame

Fight 92: Thunderzaar died to repeated Fireballs at 1:40.6 with no healing in the final 14.664-second death window. The decisive tank loss followed: Jonette died to Eye Blast at 1:59.5. Flame melee and Blaze deaths spread afterward. Uncaged Wrath appears only later, around 2:16–2:17, after the collapse was underway. Do not mistake that late buff for the first cause.

Fight 93: Tunaak died to Flame melee at 1:58.1. At 1:59.212, the Flame attacking Roarwind gained Uncaged Wrath. It then hit Roarwind for 17,311 melee damage at 2:00.913, followed by lethal Flame Blast at 2:01.245. Only a 1,037 Earth Shield heal landed between them. Jonette died at 2:12.0; Flame Blast then killed Shcherbaque, Badabuille and Moungrym together.

Rehearse the two Flame tank routes around Eye Blast and keep Flame/glaive positioning controlled. Call a lost Flame target or Uncaged Wrath immediately. Healers must cover the raid’s Fireball damage as well as the tanks. The exact movement that triggered Wrath requires replay review; the log establishes the buff and lethal burst, not a specific player’s route. The kill, fight 94, had no Uncaged Wrath application, but still had eight deaths versus one in October 22’s kill.
