Stop the first deaths on Archimonde

Archimonde Air Burst Shade of Akama Every wipe Other improvements Next raid

Eight kills and four wipes: three on Archimonde, one on Shade of Akama. The main improvements are faster curse removal and healing after Doomfire, reliable Air Burst landings, and keeping Jonette covered while he holds the Shade adds. The eventual Archimonde and Shade kills had no player deaths, so the night already contains examples of successful execution.

Reanalysed on September 15 from report HP3k7trf1Jyng2px. Times below are elapsed from each pull. The night contains 120 death-table entries, including 97 on wipes. These include cleanup and repeat deaths; they are not a count of mistakes. Every wipe was checked in chronological order against damage, healing, and relevant auras. A log does not timestamp the voice call to wipe, so later deaths are described as the collapse rather than assigned an invented call time.

Archimonde: the first two wipes began before the tank died

First wipe — fight 42, 47.02% remaining. Roarwind was the first death at 0:53.8, to Doomfire after a long overlap with Grip of the Legion. The curse was applied at 0:35.6 and removed by Kianne at 0:51.4: almost sixteen seconds later, after seven ticks of 2,479 damage. Fear was active from 0:48.4 until the death. Roarwind's last effective heal was his own Healing Touch at 0:46.8. This was a curse, fire, fear, and healing overlap; calling it simply a tank death misses the actionable sequence.

Bruand then died to Falling at 1:26.6, following Air Burst at 1:20.9. Prevaraa and Trígger died to Doomfire at 1:37.5 and 1:39.2. By the time Jonette died to Soul Charge at 1:55.3 and Madef died to melee at 1:55.6, the raid was already deep into a death chain. The old review's claim that one death's charge took the tank omitted several earlier losses. Fight 42 deaths.

Second wipe — fight 43, 59.76% remaining. Dipstick died to Doomfire at 1:05.5, followed by Klib at 1:09.7 and Toastersha at 1:13.8. Dipstick used a healing potion at 0:58.8, but had no other healing in his final eight seconds. Klib used a healthstone, then took a 4,500 Soul Charge hit alongside the burn. Toastersha had no healing in his final eight seconds. These were players who needed help after the initial fire contact, not evidence that the tank started the wipe.

Madef died at 1:38.7 as the fifteenth death-table entry. His melee death was a consequence of a raid already losing people and healing capacity. The old statement that he was the first death on this pull is incorrect. Fight 43 deaths.

Next time: assign curse coverage by area, including a backup when the assigned player is moving or feared. Call Doomfire targets for healing until their burn ends. The lingering burn continues after leaving the fire, so repeated periodic ticks alone do not prove someone kept standing in it. Keep a safe path and fear response ready before the next Fear. Each death enables another Soul Charge, which makes preventing the first loss especially valuable. Archimonde mechanics.

Archimonde: the third wipe includes a clear landing failure

Third wipe — fight 44, 22.71% remaining. Toastersha died to Doomfire at 2:02.2, and Nosaj died to Doomfire at 2:25.3. Madef was then hit by Air Burst at 2:22.4 and died to 19,074 falling damage at 2:28.1. Healing had continued during the flight, including several overheals just before landing. More healing would not have solved that fall. No successful slow-fall effect is recorded in the checked landing window; that establishes the failed protection, not whether the item was missing, mistimed, or failed to activate. Fight 44 deaths.

Next time: include Madef in the Tears check and landing call. Everyone needs an accessible Tears of the Goddess keybind and should use it close enough to landing that its protection remains active. Bruand's earlier falling death shows this is a raid-wide check. Keep healers moving out of Doomfire before they finish a cast; losing Toastersha and Nosaj also reduced the raid's recovery options on this attempt. Air Burst and Tears.

The next attempt, fight 45, was a 3:09 kill with no deaths. Keep the same survival priorities even when damage uptime drops during movement.

Shade of Akama: add pressure exceeded the healing on Jonette

Fight 80, 51.17% remaining. Jonette was the first death at 1:04.3. His final four seconds contained damage from a rogue, an elementalist, and two separate spiritbinders. Those enemies dealt 13,894 effective damage from 1:00.0 through the death, including Debilitating Poison. The last direct heal landed at 1:00.7; only a Renew tick followed before he died.

The 305-damage Spiritbinder melee that finished him is not an explanation by itself. This was accumulated add pressure and a gap in direct healing. Afterward, rogues and other adds reached Nosaj, Swellery, and Toastersha. Moungrym also took three Rain of Fire hits at 1:05.5, 1:07.5, and 1:09.5, with the last killing him. Those later losses fit the tank-loss collapse; they should not all be presented as independent pull-ending mistakes. Fight 80 deaths.

Next time: name Jonette's healer and backup, call when multiple waves accumulate, and control or kill excess adds before their pressure becomes unmanageable. Keep healing the add tanks when damage dealers switch to Shade. The encounter's tanks handle the adds while Akama handles the Shade itself. Shade strategy.

The follow-up, fight 82, was a 1:27 kill with no deaths.
